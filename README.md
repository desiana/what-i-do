# What I Do

## Marketing & Growth | Commercial Intelligence | AI-Enabled Marketing Operations

I start with a basic question: does what a business wants to sell genuinely match what the market needs?

My work moves from commercial intelligence into marketing execution and the systems behind it. I gather and analyse information from inside and outside the business, understand what it means in its commercial context, and turn it into decisions about positioning, content, digital implementation, measurement and learning.

A service-design perspective helps me understand the journey and the stakeholders involved. Lean thinking helps me decide what to prioritise. Human-in-the-loop AI and automation help me expand and accelerate the analytical and operational work without replacing human judgement.

The pattern across my work is:

**Insight → Strategy → Execution → Systems**

## Selected work

### [Commercial intelligence and AI-enabled marketing operations](case-studies/b2b-commercial-intelligence.md)

In a specialised, high-value B2B environment, I connect fragmented public evidence with subject-matter expertise and turn it into structured account intelligence, testable opportunity hypotheses and practical marketing decisions.

The work includes market and project research, account-based growth, SEO/GEO content operations, workflow governance, knowledge architecture, AI-assisted implementation and locally validated WordPress development.

**Boundary:** operating workflows and local implementations exist. Revenue, pipeline, published-content performance and production deployment are not claimed without verified evidence.

### [Consumer insight to commercial direction](case-studies/consumer-insight.md)

I combined **121 survey responses, 76 public online discussions and 402 product reviews** to understand how people choose and use household-storage products.

The findings became recommendations for product thinking, positioning, messaging, decision support and the connected online/offline buying journey.

**Boundary:** the research and recommendations were completed. Organisational adoption and commercial outcomes are not claimed.

### [Earlier full-stack digital marketing work](case-studies/full-stack-marketing.md)

My earlier work covered the complete path from audience research to digital execution: positioning, customer journeys, content, paid-media testing, lead nurturing, customer engagement, websites, funnels, e-commerce and early AI-assisted marketing.

One documented campaign comparison produced **24% greater reach, 21% more views and a 25% lower cost per follow** after the targeting and creative framing changed.

**Boundary:** this is one bounded experiment, not a general business-growth claim.


## What connects the work

I am primarily a marketer, but I work unusually close to business development.

I work upstream into commercial intelligence to understand where the opportunity may be, and downstream into marketing operations to make the work repeatable.

That means I do not begin with “let’s create content.” I begin with the demand, the commercial context, what the business can genuinely deliver, and what still needs to be tested.

## Privacy and evidence

Organizations are intentionally described by their industry and operating characteristics rather than by name.

The case studies distinguish research from adoption, recommendations from implementation, and activity from measured outcomes. Confidential material, live commercial targets, personal data, private links and client communications are excluded.
